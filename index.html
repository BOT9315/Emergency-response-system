The pipeline

From free text to a unit on scene

01
Reported

Describe it plainly

A dispatcher types what happened — no forms, no dropdowns. "Car crash on the highway, two injured" is enough to start.

02
Triaged

AI scores it instantly

The classifier assigns a type and severity, then computes a 0–100 priority score from casualties, risk, and wait time.

03
Dispatched

Nearest unit, routed

The optimizer matches the incident to the closest suitable unit — one click, or auto-dispatch the entire queue at once.
